PALM HOUSE / BROWSER DEMO

Your hotel stays with you.

This demo saves progress in your browser. There are no accounts, ads, purchases or third-party analytics. Clearing browser data can remove your hotel; export a backup in Hotel book → Settings.

Optional playtest notes

In the playtest build, you can choose to record a local log of tutorial milestones, purchases made with game cash, chapter completion, active session time, session counts, calendar dates and generic error categories. It contains no name, email, advertising identifier or free-text feedback. We keep up to 2,000 events in this browser. Nothing is uploaded automatically.

You can inspect the exported JSON, choose whether to share it with the person who invited you, or turn logging off to delete the local log. Exported copies remain wherever you saved or shared them. Please avoid adding personal information when sharing feedback.

Hosting

A public hosting provider receives normal web requests, including your IP address, to serve the game. Its own privacy policy applies to that infrastructure. The game itself does not send playtest logs to the host.

Help

For this private testing round, contact the person who sent you the invitation. A public support contact and final store privacy disclosures will be added before a commercial release.

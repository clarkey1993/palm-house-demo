PALM HOUSE / PLAYTEST

Your hotel stays with you.

The browser demo saves progress in your browser. The installed iOS playtest saves progress inside the app. There are no accounts, ads, purchases or third-party analytics. Clearing browser data can remove your browser hotel; deleting the installed app can remove its hotel. Export a backup in Hotel book → Settings.

Optional playtest notes

In the playtest build, you can choose to record a local log of tutorial milestones, purchases made with game cash, chapter completion, active session time, session counts, calendar dates and generic error categories. It contains no name, email, advertising identifier or free-text feedback. We keep up to 2,000 events in this browser. Nothing is uploaded automatically.

You can inspect the exported JSON, choose whether to share it with the person who invited you, or turn logging off to delete the local log. Exported copies remain wherever you saved or shared them. Please avoid adding personal information when sharing feedback.

Feedback

Hotel book → Settings lets you send a message without an account. When you select Send feedback, your message, game version and a random submission reference are sent to our feedback service, hosted on Sites, and stored privately for the developer. No hotel save or playtest log is attached. Please do not include personal information. The hosting provider receives normal connection information. A daily hash of the connection address is stored to limit spam; raw IP addresses are not stored in the feedback database. Messages are retained for review. Drafts stay in the current page until submitted or the page is closed.

Installed iOS playtest

The app bundles its artwork and game files for offline play. Optional local playtest logging is disabled in the iOS build. Backup export opens the system share sheet; you choose where to save or share the file. Nothing is shared automatically. TestFlight feedback and diagnostics are handled separately by Apple.

Hosting

A public hosting provider receives normal web requests, including your IP address, to serve the game. Its own privacy policy applies to that infrastructure. The game itself does not send playtest logs to the host.

Help

For this private testing round, contact the person who sent you the invitation. A public support contact and final store privacy disclosures will be added before a commercial release.
